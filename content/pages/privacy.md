# Privacy & Cookie Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

This policy explains how **aria.watch** (this website) processes personal data, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and the ePrivacy rules on cookies. We keep it short because we collect very little.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is **Aria Watch**, a small, non-commercial open-source project.

Contact for any privacy matter: [info@aria.watch](mailto:info@aria.watch).

## What this website does _not_ do

- No user accounts, no registration, no newsletter, no forms on this website.
- No advertising and no advertising or profiling cookies.
- No third-party fonts, images, videos or social-media widgets: every file is served from our own server. Links to other sites (GitHub, Instagram, AliExpress, the Aria Watch app) only take you there when you click them.
- Nothing from Google is loaded unless you accept analytics cookies.

## Data we process

### 1. Server logs

**What:** the website runs on a server we manage ourselves (with [Coolify](https://coolify.io)). When you visit a page, the web server records the request: an IP address shortened to its first part (for example `203.0.113.0` — the last block is removed before it is written), date and time, the page requested, the referring page and your browser's user agent. To deliver the page, the server necessarily handles your full IP address in transit, but does not store it in our website logs.

**Why and legal basis:** to keep the site secure and working (e.g. detecting abuse and errors) — our legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f). We don't use these logs to track or profile anyone.

**How long:** they are technical logs, kept only for troubleshooting and security; they are deleted automatically when the logs rotate or the site is redeployed.

### 2. Website analytics (Google Analytics 4) — only with your consent

**What:** if you click **Accept** on the cookie banner, we load Google Analytics 4. It sets the cookies listed below and collects pseudonymous usage data: pages viewed, time on page, approximate location (country/city derived from your IP address — Google Analytics 4 does not store the IP address itself), device type, browser and referring site.

We configured it to collect the minimum: Google Signals and advertising features are **off**, no data is used for ad personalisation, cookies expire after 13 months, and analytics data is kept for **2 months**.

**Why and legal basis:** to understand which pages are useful and improve the site — your consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code (ePrivacy). If you reject or ignore the banner, Google Analytics is never loaded.

**Who receives it:** Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, acting as our data processor. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the United States, which is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission of 10 July 2023); Standard Contractual Clauses also apply. See [how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Withdrawing consent:** at any time, via **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing stops analytics immediately and deletes the analytics cookies from your browser. It does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew.

### 3. When you email us

**What:** your email address, name (if given) and whatever you write.

**Why and legal basis:** to answer you — taking steps at your request / our legitimate interest in replying, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f).

**How long:** as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, then deleted.

## Cookies

We use the following cookies and similar technologies. Essential ones do not require consent; analytics ones are only set after you accept.

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Duration | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `aw-consent` (local storage) | aria.watch | Remembers your cookie choice | 6 months, then you are asked again | Essential |
| `theme` (local storage) | aria.watch | Remembers light/dark mode | Until you clear it | Essential (preference) |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes visitors | 13 months | Analytics — consent only |
| `_ga_<ID>` | Google Analytics | Keeps session state | 13 months | Analytics — consent only |

The essential items are stored only in your browser and are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can also delete cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15);
- have it **corrected** (Art. 16) or **erased** (Art. 17);
- **restrict** its processing (Art. 18);
- **data portability** (Art. 20);
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21);
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting earlier processing (Art. 7(3)).

To exercise them, write to [info@aria.watch](mailto:info@aria.watch). We will reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Italy the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it), or the authority of the EU country where you live or work.

## Other things you should know

- **No automated decisions:** we make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling.
- **Providing data is voluntary:** you can use the whole website without accepting analytics cookies.
- **Children:** this website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their data.
- **External sites:** when you follow a link to GitHub, Instagram, AliExpress or app.aria.watch, that site's own privacy policy applies.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how we process data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the change concerns cookies, the cookie banner will ask for your choice again.
